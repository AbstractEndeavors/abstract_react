After enjoying all the delights of what he believed

had never left the side of the Grand Master but had

merely experienced a foretaste of the paradise that

In this matter the Assassins established a system of
organized murder on a basis of religious fervor, and
unleashed a reign of terror throughout the East. Years
later, in secret records released by two of their own
leaders, a list was found of celebrated men of all nations
who had been their victims.

Eventually, the leaders of the Assassins turned on
each other and became the victims of the poisons and
daggers they had prepared for others. Although the
dynasty of the Assassins had ended by 1250, the sects
from which they derived continued.
